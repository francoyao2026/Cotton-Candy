Our company

Built for smart retail, automated convenience, and memorable customer experiences.

Rockstream Automation Inc. is a trusted partner for commercial automation and automated vending solutions in Western Canada. We specialize in innovative devices and smart vending systems, including automated cotton candy machines and interactive concession technology.

Business profile

Professional, dependable equipment support from install to maintenance.

Headquartered in Calgary, Alberta, Rockstream Automation Inc. delivers complete end-to-end support across the full lifecycle of every machine. From initial installation and equipment supply to routine refills, cleaning, maintenance, and technical repairs, our team helps ensure smooth, hassle-free operations for every client.

We partner directly with shopping malls, entertainment centers, and high-traffic commercial venues to deliver engaging visitor experiences, increase convenience, and create new revenue opportunities.

  • Commercial automation and smart vending solutions
  • Automated cotton candy vending and concession systems
  • Full support from deployment through ongoing service
  • Retail and entertainment venue partnerships across Western Canada and North America
